# Glossary

English-first glossary with Malay-friendly explanations.

## Harness Engineering

A way to put reusable structure around AI work so it becomes easier to repeat, review, and improve.

Malay-friendly: macam pasang "tali keselamatan" pada kerja AI supaya tidak lari terlalu jauh.

## Rabbit Hole

When a task keeps expanding and you lose the original goal.

Malay-friendly: bila kerja masuk lubang yang makin dalam, sampai susah nak balik ke tujuan asal.

## Vibing

Exploring ideas with AI in a loose, creative way.

Malay-friendly: ikut flow, cuba-cuba idea dengan AI.

## Unmanaged Vibing

Exploring without enough goal, context, checkpoints, or done criteria.

Malay-friendly: ikut flow tanpa pagar, jadi AI mudah bawa kita ke arah lain.

## Context

The background information AI needs to understand your work.

Malay-friendly: maklumat latar belakang, istilah, peraturan, dan cara kerja sebenar.

## Context Pack

A small set of files that explain a domain, task, examples, prompts, and checkpoints.

Malay-friendly: folder rujukan untuk AI faham kerja kita.

## Checkpoint

A planned stop where the AI summarizes progress, remaining work, and risk.

Malay-friendly: tempat berhenti sekejap untuk semak hala tuju.

## Done Criteria

The conditions that tell you the task is finished enough.

Malay-friendly: tanda kerja sudah cukup siap, bukan sempurna tanpa penghujung.

## Ask-Before-Action Rule

A rule that tells the AI when to ask before making a big change.

Malay-friendly: peraturan supaya AI tanya dulu sebelum terus bina atau ubah benda besar.

## Prompt Library

A reusable collection of prompts, templates, examples, and instructions.

Malay-friendly: koleksi arahan yang boleh guna semula.
