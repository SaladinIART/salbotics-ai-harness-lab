# Checkpoint Rule

Use this rule whenever AI work starts to become multi-step.

```text
After each major step, summarize:
1. What changed
2. What remains
3. Where risk is
```

## Why This Works

Checkpoints stop drift early. They give the human a chance to correct direction before the AI creates too much extra work.

## Copy-Paste Version

```text
Checkpoint rule:
After each major step, stop and summarize what changed, what remains, and where risk is. Do not continue to the next major step until I confirm or adjust the direction.
```
