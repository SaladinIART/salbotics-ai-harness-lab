# Context Pack Starter

Create one folder for one repeated workflow.

```text
context-pack/
  README.md
  DOMAIN.md
  PROMPTS.md
  EXAMPLES.md
  CHECKPOINTS.md
  GEMINI.md
```

## README.md

What this pack is for, who should use it, and what output it should produce.

## DOMAIN.md

Terms, assumptions, safety rules, constraints, and domain-specific judgment.

## PROMPTS.md

Reusable prompts. Keep each prompt tied to a clear job.

## EXAMPLES.md

Good outputs, bad outputs, and why they pass or fail.

## CHECKPOINTS.md

Review gates. Example:

```text
After each major step, summarize:
1. What changed
2. What remains
3. Where risk is
```

## GEMINI.md

Language-oriented handoff: terminology, summary notes, audience context, and continuation notes.
